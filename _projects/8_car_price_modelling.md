Work from my data analyst internship at TransOrg Analytics (May – Aug 2023, remote).

I analysed client data from the Indian automotive market to find pricing trends and support
pricing strategy. I used SQL to prepare the data, built an interactive Tableau dashboard on
top of it, and trained XGBoost and gradient-boosting models to predict car prices.
