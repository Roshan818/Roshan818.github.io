I build data infrastructure at [SCA Technologies](https://scanalytics.com), where I work on supply-chain analytics for large restaurant and retail networks. Day to day that means PySpark and Databricks pipelines, fact-table design, and the Tableau reporting that sits on top of them. I joined in 2024 as a trainee analyst and now lead this work as a Senior Business Analyst.

I studied Civil Engineering at IIT Guwahati, but spent most of it somewhere else — building the vision stack for our Mars rover team, competing in Inter-IIT ML contests, and eventually writing a thesis on state and parameter estimation for stochastic systems on Riemannian manifolds. That thesis is where my current interests come from.

These days I'm working toward research in **robotics and learning for control** — specifically the estimation side: how systems infer their own state under noise, and how learned policies hold up when that estimate is wrong. I reimplement papers in this area and write up what I find, including the parts that don't reproduce. Those writeups are on the [blog](/blog/), and the code is in [projects](/projects/).
